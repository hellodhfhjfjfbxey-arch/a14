What the content on Cozysok is, and what it is not.

Personal style

Styling suggestions are ideas, not rules. Choose clothes that suit your body, comfort, budget and taste, and consider sensitivities to particular fibres or finishes.

Clothing advice is full of rules that are really just somebody’s taste stated firmly. We try to separate the two: facts about cloth, construction and fit on one side, and preference on the other. What suits you depends on your body, your climate, your life and what you enjoy wearing, none of which a website can see. Treat everything here as a starting point to adapt rather than instructions to follow, and ignore anything that does not work for you.

Interactive tools

The sock size converter and cosy matcher offer general, illustrative guidance only. Shoe fit, gait and construction vary, nothing here is medical advice, and a blister that is painful or will not heal should be seen by a pharmacist or a doctor.

The tools run entirely in your browser, which means your entries are never sent to us and nothing is stored on our side. They work from typical values and ordinary assumptions, so they give you a sensible starting point rather than a precise answer for your own situation. Treat the results as a guide, check them against the instructions supplied with whatever you are actually using, and use your own judgement. If a tool gives you a result that looks wrong, please tell us, because that is usually how we find a mistake.

Care guidance

Washing and drying suggestions are general. Always follow the care label on your garments. We are not responsible for shrinkage, colour loss or other damage.

Care advice is general because garments are not. Two items in the same fibre can behave differently depending on the weave, the finish, the dye and how they were made. The label sewn into your garment was written by the people who made it and always takes priority over anything here. When you are unsure, test on a hidden seam first, start with the gentlest method and work up, and accept that a professional cleaner is cheaper than replacing something you ruined.

Every image carries alternative text describing what it shows, so the page still makes sense with images turned off or read aloud by a screen reader. Any logos or branding that happen to appear within a photograph belong to their owners.

No guarantee of results

Everything on cozysok.com is general information published in good faith. We cannot promise that following it will produce any particular result for you, because circumstances, products and conditions differ from person to person.

Two people can follow the same guide and get different results, because equipment, materials, conditions and starting points all differ. That is normal and it is not a sign that either of you did anything wrong. We try to tell you what a good result looks like and what to check along the way, rather than promising a number. Where a result really matters to you, test on something small or unimportant first.

How current the information is
